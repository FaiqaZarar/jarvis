# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Jarvis to be a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone, whatever their age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). The summary below is not a replacement for it.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Taking responsibility for our mistakes and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews) and when someone officially represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to any of the maintainers listed in [AUTHORS](AUTHORS), through their GitHub profiles. All reports are reviewed promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy is respected.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that don't follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour they judge inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful. The full enforcement guidelines are in the Contributor Covenant linked above.
