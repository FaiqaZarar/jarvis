# Vision

## Why Jarvis exists

People keep what they know in many places: folders on their laptop, Google Drive, notes apps. An AI assistant is only as useful as what it can see, but handing all of that to a cloud service means giving up control of it.

Jarvis takes the other path. It brings your files together in **one private library on your own computer**, organizes them automatically, and lets Claude (or any MCP client) use that library while showing exactly where each answer came from.

## Principles

1. **Local first.** Your files, the index and the topic tree stay on your machine. Anything that leaves it is opt-in, documented and minimal.
2. **Answers you can check.** Every answer can be traced back to the files and passages behind it.
3. **Organized without effort.** Topics come from the content itself; you never have to tag or file anything.
4. **Calm software.** Plain language, progress you can see, failures paired with the one action that fixes them, and nothing destructive without a confirmation.
5. **Open and swappable.** Connectors, embedding providers and clients plug in behind small, documented interfaces.

## Where we're heading

These are directions, not promises or dates. Discussion and help are welcome on any of them.

- **Installers and updates:** signed installers for Windows, macOS and Linux on every release, with in-app updates.
- **More sources:** a Notion connector, and more file types such as PowerPoint and Excel.
- **Better search for natural questions:** whole-sentence questions should work as well as keywords do today.
- **Stronger protection at rest:** optional encryption of the local library, beyond relying on disk encryption.
- **More clients:** first-class setup for MCP clients beyond Claude Desktop.

## Not planned

- A hosted, cloud-only version that stores your library on someone else's servers.
- Features that require an account just to use your own files.
